All color tokens are CSS custom properties declared in `apps/web/src/ui/styles/global/index.css` (light values in `:root`, dark overrides in the `[data-theme='dark']` block). The swatches below read the variables live, so they always show the current values in your active theme; flip Starlight's toggle to see the [dark mode](/design-system/foundations/dark-mode/) set.

## Surfaces and the frame

Panel backgrounds come in warmth steps plus an inset alias, and `--frame` is the ink used for every hard border and brutal shadow:

<TokenSwatch tokens={[
  '--surface-panel',
  '--surface-panel-alt',
  '--surface-panel-soft',
  '--surface-panel-inset',
  '--frame',
]} />

`--frame` is the pivot of the whole system: it is cream-on-ink in light mode and ink-on-cream in dark mode, and because every `--shadow-brutal-*` token references it, the [shadows](/design-system/foundations/shadows/) re-theme automatically.

## Washes

A brand hue tinted into a surface, toward white in light and toward black in dark, with the ink that reads on it. `Banner`, `MetricCard` and the planner's stat cards draw with them (`bg-wash-teal`, `text-wash-teal-icon`). A wash's `-title` and `-badge-ink` are the text that sits on it and on its `-badge`, each at 4.5:1 or better in both themes, which `contrast.test.ts` asserts for every scheme `Banner` and `MetricCard` use:

## Calendar days

The fills the calendar paints its days with, and the numerals on them. A Holiday is an ink tile with white numerals in light and a cream tile with ink numerals in dark; a Custom Holiday is the same tile with a `--day-custom-ring` dashed ring inside it; a weekend sits on `--muted` with `--muted-foreground` numerals; an Alternative preview is a yellow wash. Suggested and Manual Days are the brand teal. ## Fixed colors and overlays

White on a solid fill, black taken at an alpha, the hairline between list rows and the error page's console:

<TokenSwatch tokens={[
  '--on-fill',
  '--shade',
  '--divider',
  '--success',
  '--terminal-bg',
  '--terminal-text',
]} />

`--divider` is black at 15% in light and the frame at 15% in dark; a card in a brand color does not flip, so its rows keep `border-shade/15`. A consumer that cannot read a custom property (the Stripe iframe, the skeletons, the PDF, the confetti, the email template) takes named constants from `apps/web/src/ui/styles/palette.ts`, which a test holds equal to the tokens it repeats, and the email template from `apps/web/src/application/email/palette.ts`; no other file of the app writes a literal color, generated code included.

## Gradients and effects

Two tokens hold full gradient values rather than single colors:

<TokenSwatch tokens={[
  '--page-glow',
  '--grid-color',
]} />

`--page-glow` is the set of faint radial gradients painted behind the page body (`apps/web/src/ui/styles/base/index.css` sets it as a fixed `background-image`); `--grid-color` is the near-transparent ink used for background grids.

## How the semantic layer maps to Tailwind

Separate files, separate jobs:

- `apps/web/src/ui/styles/global/index.css` declares the raw values (`:root` and `[data-theme='dark']`).
- `apps/web/src/ui/styles/theme/index.css` re-exposes a subset under Tailwind's `@theme inline` block: `--color-background: var(--background)`, `--color-accent: var(--accent)`, and so on.

Only the tokens aliased inside `@theme inline` become Tailwind utilities (`bg-background`, `text-muted-foreground`, `border-border`, `ring-ring`, and the role tokens: `text-positive`, `bg-wash-teal`, `border-divider`…). Everything else, meaning brand colors, surfaces, `--frame` and shadows, is consumed through arbitrary values, e.g. `bg-[var(--surface-panel)]`, `border-[var(--frame)]`, `shadow-[var(--shadow-brutal-md)]`. When adding a component, prefer the semantic utilities and reach for `var()` arbitraries only for surfaces and frames, matching the existing primitives in `apps/web/src/ui/modules/core/primitives/`.
