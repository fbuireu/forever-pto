The suggestion-engine terms below are defined in code in `apps/web/src/domain/calendar/types.ts` and explained in depth in `apps/web/src/domain/calendar/AGENTS.md`.

### PTO Day

One day from the user's annual PTO budget: a unit of budget, not a date on the calendar, until a Suggestion places it (`ptoDays` in the filters store, `apps/web/src/application/stores/filters.ts`). Everything the engine does is about spending this budget as efficiently as possible.

### Bridge

A block of PTO days that connects weekends and public holidays into one contiguous stretch off. A single PTO day on a Monday before a Tuesday holiday "bridges" the weekend to the holiday, yielding four Effective Days for one PTO Day spent. Modeled by the `Bridge` interface: `ptoDaysNeeded`, `effectiveDays`, `efficiency` and the concrete `ptoDays` dates. A Bridge is the unit the planner reasons about; the individual dates inside it are PTO Days, and there is no separate name for them.

### Effective Day

One day of the continuous stretch off a Bridge or Suggestion produces. The total counts PTO days plus the weekends and holidays they lean on, each once, however many Bridges reach it. **Efficiency** is `effectiveDays / ptoDaysNeeded`; each Strategy refuses a Bridge whose marginal Efficiency, the days it adds to the plan so far per PTO Day, falls under its floor (tunables in `apps/web/src/domain/calendar/const.ts`).

### Strategy

The rule that decides which Bridges make it into a Suggestion when the budget cannot cover them all. Its values, exported as the `FilterStrategy` const object in `apps/web/src/domain/calendar/types.ts`:

- **`grouped`** grows a few long blocks, up to about two weeks each, accepting a lower return per PTO Day to get there. Fewer, longer breaks.
- **`optimized`** takes whatever adds the most Effective Days per PTO Day to the plan so far. The most Effective Days for the budget.
- **`balanced`** gives every quarter its share of the budget and prefers breaks of up to a week. Breaks across the whole year.

### Suggestion

The engine's output for a given budget, calendar and strategy: the set of recommended PTO dates plus the bridges and metrics behind them (`Suggestion` interface, produced by `apps/web/src/domain/calendar/suggestions/generateSuggestions.ts`). Users can hand-edit the suggested days afterwards.

### Alternative

A genuinely distinct plan for the same inputs, produced by `apps/web/src/domain/calendar/alternatives/generateAlternatives.ts` from the other Strategies and from the chosen one re-run without one of the Suggestion's breaks, each far enough from every other plan to be a real choice and never with more Effective Days or a higher Efficiency than the Suggestion, so the user can compare plans rather than accept a single answer.

### Carry-over Months

Months of the following year included in the planning window (`carryOverMonths` in the filters store). It lets a plan spill unused PTO past the year boundary, for example bridging the winter holidays into January.

### Premium

The tier unlocked by a Donation. Its key is the Stripe PaymentIntent id, surfaced as `premiumKey` (see `apps/web/src/application/use-cases/activatePremium.ts` and the premium store `apps/web/src/application/stores/premium.ts`). The active session itself is carried by the `premium-token` JWT cookie; see [cookies](/reference/cookies/).
